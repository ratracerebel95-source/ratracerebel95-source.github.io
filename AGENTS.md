# Sahulat Studio site: rules for Hive agents
- Static HTML only, using /assets/style.css. Copy the header and footer from index.html.
- New guide: /guides/<slug>.html; add a card between GUIDES:START/END in guides/index.html, add it to the home page "Latest guides" (keep 6 newest), and add its URL to sitemap.xml.
- New product: a card between PRODUCTS:START/END in products/index.html, linking to its live store URL.
- Quality bar: genuinely useful, original, accurate, people-first. No keyword stuffing, no thin or duplicate pages, no fake reviews, no invented statistics. At most 2 new guides per day.
- Every guide links naturally to at most 1-2 relevant products.
